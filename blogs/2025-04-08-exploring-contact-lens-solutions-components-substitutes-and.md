This article explores what is contact lens solution made of, examines contact solution ingredients, and answers common questions such as "Is saline solution the same as contact solution?" and "Can you use contact lens solution as eye drops?" What Is Contact Lens Solution? Contact lens solution is a specially formulated liquid used to clean, disinfect, and store contact lenses.
