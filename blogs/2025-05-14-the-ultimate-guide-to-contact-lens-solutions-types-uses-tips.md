Contact lens solution is a daily necessity for lens wearers. More than just a cleaner, it serves to disinfect, hydrate, and store lenses. Different types of contact lenses-soft, hard, and gas permeable-require different types of care, and the solution's ingredients (e.g., preservatives, boric acid) can significantly impact comfort and eye health.
